Mechanic

Repairs, tuning, and towing for the whole city.

Open

Mechanic work is open once you are verified. Repair, tune, and tow for the city. You do not need a separate department application to start.

What to know

  • Finish Discord verification, then connect.
  • Keep the work in character. Take out-of-character disputes to a report (C5, C8).
  • A working microphone is required (C7).

Where to start

Ask in Discord if you want to join an existing shop, or build the work into your own civilian story.
