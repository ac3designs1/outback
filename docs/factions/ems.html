EMS

Respond to calls and patch the city back together.

Whitelisted

EMS is a whitelisted department. Medics answer calls and keep injured people in the medical scene instead of skipping straight back to the street.

Before you apply

  • Finish Discord verification so you can connect to the server.
  • Characters must be 18 or older, and you need a clear working microphone (C6, C7).
  • Stay in character while you treat someone. Disputes go to a report (C5, C8).

On the job

  • Respond to calls and roleplay treatment. Severe injuries need a realistic reaction (F5).
  • New Life Rule applies after death unless emergency services properly revive the player (F4).
  • Emergency service equipment cannot be stolen (F16).

How to apply

Applications are posted in the OutbackRP Discord.
