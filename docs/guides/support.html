Seeking Support

Pick the right channel so you get an answer faster.

Using the right support path gets you answered faster. Start with the option that matches your situation.

In-game issueAn active rule break or a situation that needs staff right now. Use /report in chat. Discord ticketReports, whitelist, appeals, or no staff online. Open a ticket in Discord. Technical fixConnection, textures, cache, or account linking. Try a technical guide first.

In-game support

For active situations that need staff attention while you are in the server.

1

Use /report

Type /report and follow the prompts. Include the player ID, what happened, and when. Use /report again to reopen the report and chat with staff.
Staff availability
Staff are not online all day. If nobody responds in game, open a Discord ticket.

Discord support

For reports when staff are offline, whitelist, appeals, and general questions.

2

Open a ticket

Join discord.gg/outbackrp and open a ticket. Pick the type that matches the problem so it reaches the right people.
3

Include evidence

Video or screenshots speed reports up. Reports go through official channels. Do not handle a punishment yourself (C5).

Technical issues

Most connection and client problems can be fixed without staff. Try these before opening a ticket:

  • Link your Cfx.re account
  • Clear FiveM cache
  • Fix texture loss
  • Stuck character

Store questions go through Discord. The packages are listed on the store page.
