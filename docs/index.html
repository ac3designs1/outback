Welcome to OutbackRP

The documentation hub for OutbackRP, a casual semi-serious FiveM server in Melbourne.

OutbackRP is a Melbourne FiveM roleplay community. These docs cover the server rules, in-game guides, technical fixes, and department handbooks.

Read before you play
By connecting to OutbackRP or joining the Discord, you agree to follow the rules. Staff may act on behaviour that breaks the spirit of a rule, not just its wording.

About the server

The server is allowlisted. Join discord.gg/outbackrp, read the rules, and complete verification before you connect. Direct connect: connect cfx.re/join/y994rg5

Police and EMS are whitelisted. Mechanic and civilian jobs are open. Scheduled restarts are 8am and 3pm. The city holds up to 200 players.
