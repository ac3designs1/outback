Police

Patrol, investigate, and keep the peace.

Whitelisted

Police is a whitelisted department. Officers patrol, investigate, and keep the peace across Melbourne.

Before you apply

  • Finish Discord verification so you can connect to the server.
  • Characters must be 18 or older, and you need a clear working microphone (C6, C7).
  • Stay in character on duty. Take out-of-character problems to a report, not the middle of a scene (C5, C8).

On the job

  • Patrol, investigate, and give people a fair chance to respond in a scene.
  • Emergency service equipment cannot be stolen (F16).
  • Safe zones and whitelisted businesses are protected. Hostile actions are not permitted there (F17).

How to apply

Applications are posted in the OutbackRP Discord. Read the community rules and FiveM rules before you send one.
