OutbackRP

Link Cfx.re Account

You must be signed in through the FiveM client to join OutbackRP.

Required
You must be signed in to a FiveM (Cfx.re) account through your FiveM client before you can connect to OutbackRP.

Creating a Cfx.re account

  1. Go to forum.cfx.re.
  2. Click Sign Up.
  3. Create an account, or sign in with Google or Patreon.

Signing in via the FiveM client

1

Launch FiveM

Open the FiveM client from your desktop or Start menu.
2

Link account

In the top-right of the FiveM home screen, click Link Account, then select Sign in with Cfx.re.
3

Authorize

Log in in your web browser and click Authorize. Your browser should redirect back to the FiveM client once linked.
Rockstar account
FiveM also requires a linked Rockstar Social Club account tied to your copy of GTA V. Make sure GTA V is fully updated and that you have launched it at least once through Steam, Epic, or the Rockstar Launcher before using FiveM.
