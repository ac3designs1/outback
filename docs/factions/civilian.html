Civilian

Delivery, taxi, businesses, and whatever your story needs.

Open

Civilian life is open to everyone who has finished Discord verification. Delivery, taxi, businesses, and personal stories all live here.

What to know

  • Value your character's life and act like you want to survive (F3).
  • Don't attack or run people over without a roleplay reason (F6, F7).
  • Give other people a fair chance to respond. Scenes are not about always winning (F2, F11).
  • Take out-of-character problems to a report (C5, C8).

Where to start

Connect after verification, pick a job in the city, and build from there. If you want a whitelisted department instead, look at police or EMS.
