Community Rules

These rules apply everywhere: in game, in Discord, and in voice.

C1

Treat all players with respect. Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, slurs, or personal attacks are strictly prohibited.

C2

Respect the staff team. Do not argue, abuse, impersonate, or disrespect staff members. Use proper tickets for disputes.

C3

Cheating, exploiting, hacking, mod packs, macros, or abusing glitches is strictly prohibited and may result in a permanent ban.

C4

Sexual harassment, explicit content, self-harm themes, or inappropriate roleplay is not allowed.

C5

Report rule breaks through official channels. Do not witch-hunt, retaliate, or handle punishments yourself.

C6

All characters must be 18+. Characters may not be portrayed as minors through voice, appearance, or behaviour.

C7

Use a clear working microphone. Voice communication is required unless approved otherwise.

C8

Do not break character unless staff has clearly requested OOC communication.

C9

Advertising other servers, communities, or services is prohibited.

Report rule breaks through official channels. Do not retaliate or handle punishments yourself (C5). Open a ticket in Discord.
