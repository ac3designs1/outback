How to Join

Get from a fresh install to the city in three steps.

You'll need a legal copy of GTA V on PC and the free FiveM client from fivem.net.

1. Install FiveM

  1. Own a legal copy of GTA V on PC.
  2. Download the FiveM client from fivem.net and let it update.
  3. Create and link a Cfx.re account when FiveM asks you to sign in.

2. Join Discord and verify

OutbackRP is allowlisted. Join the Discord, read the rules, grab your roles, and complete verification. You need that done before the server will let you in.

Open Discord

3. Connect

Use Connect, search “OutbackRP” in the server list, or press F8 in FiveM and run:

connect cfx.re/join/y994rg5
