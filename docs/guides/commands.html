Common Commands

Searchable reference for chat commands.

Type commands in chat (T or Enter). Bracketed commands need your own text.

Staff and support

/report

Report a rule violation or issue to online staff. Use /report again to reopen your report and chat with staff.

/ids

View other player IDs. Only for reporting rule breaks, not general use.

/myid

Display your own player ID.

/timer

Check the remaining death timer during crew, faction, or police hostilities.

/help

Open the in-game guide book.

Fixes and utilities

/fixme

Fix your character if you are stuck, frozen, or falling through the map.

/fixtextures

Reload textures if they appear blurry or fail to load.

Roleplay

/me [action]

Display an action above your character. Example: /me checks the engine

/do [description]

Describe the surroundings or a situation. Example: /do The engine smells burnt

/e [emote]

Perform an emote animation. Example: /e wave

Vehicle and interaction

/engine

Toggle your vehicle engine on or off.

/seat [number]

Switch to a specific vehicle seat, typically 1–4. Example: /seat 2

/carry

Carry another player.

/outtrunk

Exit the trunk of a vehicle.

Command misuse
Misusing commands such as /ids outside of reporting is a rule violation. Only use staff-related commands when you have a legitimate reason.
