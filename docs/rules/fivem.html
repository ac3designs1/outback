FiveM Rules

Roleplay standards for everyone in the city.

F1

Metagaming is prohibited. Do not use information your character has not learned in roleplay, including streams, Discord, or other characters.

F2

Powergaming is prohibited. Do not abuse mechanics, force unrealistic actions, or perform actions your character could not realistically do.

F3

FearRP is required. Value your character's life and act realistically when threatened or injured.

F4

New Life Rule applies after death. You cannot remember events surrounding your death unless properly revived by emergency services.

F5

Roleplay your injuries realistically. Severe injuries require realistic reactions and medical roleplay.

F6

RDM (Random Deathmatch) is prohibited. Do not kill players without proper roleplay or escalation.

F7

VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch) is prohibited. Do not use vehicles as weapons.

F8

Combat logging is prohibited. Do not disconnect to avoid roleplay situations, chases, or consequences.

F9

FailRP is prohibited. Always maintain realistic and immersive roleplay.

F10

Baiting police, gangs, or players to force interactions without proper roleplay is prohibited.

F11

"Must Win" mentality is not allowed. Roleplay is about creating scenarios, not always winning.

F12

Real Money Trading (RMT) is prohibited. Do not trade in-game items, money, or assets for real-world currency.

F13

Do not use real-world criminal organisations, extremist groups, or hate groups in roleplay.

F14

Trolling, disruptive behaviour, or intentionally ruining roleplay experiences is prohibited.

F15

Robberies must have meaningful roleplay behind them. Do not repeatedly target players without reason.

F16

Emergency service equipment cannot be stolen.

F17

Safe zones and whitelisted businesses are protected areas. Hostile actions are not permitted.

F18

Robbery, kidnapping, or hostile actions must have proper roleplay justification.

F19

You may not carry players on motorcycles or while driving vehicles.
