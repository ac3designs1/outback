Fix Texture Loss

Blurry textures, pop-in, and missing surfaces in the city.

Texture loss happens when GTA V or FiveM fails to load surfaces correctly. Try these steps in order. Most players fix it without staff.

In-game fixes

1

Use /fixtextures

While in the server, type /fixtextures in chat. This reloads textures without disconnecting.
2

Lower extended texture budget

In GTA V graphics settings, reduce Extended Texture Budget if your graphics card has limited memory. Setting it too high on a lower-end GPU often causes texture loss.

Client fixes

3

Clear FiveM cache

Corrupted cache files often cause texture issues that stick around. Follow the cache clearing guide and reconnect.
4

Update GPU drivers

Update your graphics drivers from NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel's site.
Still broken?
If textures are still missing, open a Discord ticket with your GPU model, FiveM version, and a screenshot.
